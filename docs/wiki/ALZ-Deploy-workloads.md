# Deploy workloads into the Azure foundations

At this point you have the necessary platform setup and Azure foundations (subscriptions) created and placed into their respective management groups, being secure, governed, monitored, and enabled for autonomy and are ready for your application teams to do workload deployments, migrations, and net-new development to their Azure foundations.

The following workloads outlined here provides best-practices, and curated deployment experiences for your application teams to successfully deploy them into their Azure foundations (online, corp).

Please head to the Azure Architecture Page called [Deploy Azure foundations](https://aka.ms/alz/aac) for more information and links to workload accelerators and much more.
